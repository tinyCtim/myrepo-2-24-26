usish: (adj.) From 'us', the combined objective personal pronoun used to
refer to the person speaking along with his / her quiet companion, and
the suffix '-ish', meaning typical of or resembling.  Representing a
narrow range of specific interests that tap into the us, and what we are
kinda about.

Example:  Making the inviting space more usish, we turned the French
lyrics a bit louder and soaked in the music suffusing about the room.

Or...

Soaking in the French fusion love song, the lovers declared it to be
quite 'usish', painting imagery and emotion that they alone held.

Or...

As Legano was warmly welcomed into the company of friends, the variety
of characters grew more usish, each holding unique representations of
the lovers and their quiet world.

Leave from 24 East Cedar Street and go to Newington Center. Round Carvel on sidewalk and run South past stores till you get to Northwood Road. Left on Northwood Road. Follow Northwood to intersecton at Centerwood.

  


Map 2

   Map 1     Map 3     Map 4  
Include the cul-de-sac at the end of Centerwood. Run on Ivy Lane to Forest Drive. Take a left on Forest Drive. Run to the cul-de-sac and then return to the intersection of Ivy Lane and Forest Drive. Keep going straight up the hill and then down till you get to Goodale Drive. Take a left on Goodale Drive and shortly thereafter take a left on Cedarwood Lane. Climb Cedarwood Lane (aka Newington 'Nihilator). At the top turn around and run back down the hill. Take a left on Goodale Drive and run up the slight incline till you get to East Robbins. Take a right on East Robbins.

Map 3

Run on East Robbins till it becomes Robbins and then run all the way to the 2nd entry to the Indian Hill U-shaped loop. Run the Indian Hill U-shaped loop till you get to the 1st entrance on Robbins. Take a left on Robbins and run till you get to Walsh. Go left on Walsh. Run the straight, flat route till you get to Main Street.

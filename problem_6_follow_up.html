The question was this:

What is sin-1 (1/3).



You can use your TI calculator to do this. Use the MODE key and put it
in degree mode. Then click the blue key labeled 2ND. Then hit the SIN
key. Then type the fraction 1/3. Then hit the ENTER key. You should see
the answer to the question, namely 19.47122 etc. This is saying the
inverse sine of 1/3 is about 19 degrees. Another way of saying this is
the sin(19.47122) is 1/3 which is about .333.



BTW, this is a reasonable answer. For the following reason. 19.47122 degrees
is less than 30 degrees. You know sin(30 degrees). It's 1/2, which
is .5. .333 is less than .5. As the angle increases from 0 to 90 the sin
increases from 0 to 1.

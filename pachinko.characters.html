names of characters in Pachinko

followed by a few remarks to help understand some relationships / facts - comments by lrb

0. Hoonie - twisted foot, cleft palate, husband of Yangjin, father of
Sunja, eventually dies

1. Yoseb - Isak's brother, Kyunghee's husband, gets Nagasaki job

2. Sunja - daughter of Hoonie and Yangjin, "seduced" by Hansu, becomes
kimchi / candy peddler, mother of Noa / Mozasu by Hansu / Isak 

3. Koh Hansu - "rescued' / "seduced" Sunja, wealthy / unscrupulous businessman, married
to Mieko, father of Noa

4. Isak Baek - Pastor, husband of Sunja, biological father of Mozasu,
Yoseb's brother, had TB, jailed, eventually dies

5. Kim Chengho - owned restaurant next to station - wanted to move to N.
Korea w/ Kyunghee, given a prostitute by Hansu

6. Noa - son of Sunja, eventually commits suicide 

7. Mozasu - 1/2 brother of Noa

8. Pastor Yoo - Isak's employer

9. Sexton Hu

10. Yangjin - wife of Hoonie,  eventually dies

11. Daisuke - "disabled" child of ...

12. Hoji-san

13. Haruki - friend of Noa, gay, police academy

14. Chiyaki - female friend of ..

15. Goro - pachinko owner, bought lots of clothes from Totoyama, gives
job to Mozasu

16. Kyunghee - Yoseb's wife, "courted" by Kim Chengho

17. Akiko Fumeki - at school with Noa, racial tensions, eventually
dropped by Noa

18. Yumi - wife of Mozasu, met at seamstress's shop, mother was a
prostitute, mother of Solomon, dies in car crash while saving her son

19. Mieko - Hansu's wife, eventually dies

20. Solomon - son of Yumi and Mozasu, seduced by Hana

21. Risa - wife of Noa, filing clerk at Cosmos pachinko

22. Ayama - Haruki's wife, is "barren"

23. Etsuko Nagatomi - Mozasu's girlfriend, Hana's mother, Japanese

24. Hana - daughter of Etsuko

25. Phoebe - Solomon's girlfriend, eventually splits from him

26. Kazu - Solomon's boss (who is eventually fired)

27. Giancarlo - poker player

28. Pastor Shin - marries Isak and Sunja (big on forgiving her "sin")

29. Totoyama - seamstress, Haruki's mother

hackronym n. From hack and acronym. A word that is part acronym, part abbreviation and part suffix. Example: geosynclined. Please note: All three parts must be present. Since an acronym is already a form of abbreviation, it's OK to have only an acronym part and a suffix part. Which I guess means we'll have to credit Amy Hughes with coining the World's 0th hackronym when she came up with The Abston Church of Christ (Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene is what LEGOS are made from). But at least one letter must be omitted from a legitimate abbreviation for the candidate to acquire any semblance of acronymitude and thus become a hackronym. It's worth noting that there's no need to cry over the lost letters because, for example, the loss of the "l" to get geosynclined from geolsynclined, where Geol, which "hardened" is Gol, is a legitimate abbreviation of Goliathic, made 4-"l" llllamas possible. Lebsterberg's Uncertainty Principle

The inequality

Uncertainty in Knowledge * Uncertainty in Letters > Rank's constant

In other words, the less unsure our Knowledge of something is, the more Letters we need to describe it. The precise value of Rank's constant is not known (because there aren't enough numbers to write it). For a marginally related concept, please see The Law of the Conservation of Letters.
